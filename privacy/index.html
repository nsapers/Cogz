Privacy policy

Version 1.0, build 10. Updated 2026-09-24.

Operator and contact

Nayan Sapers operates Cogz. Email paperssapers@gmail.com for privacy or support questions.

What stays on your iPhone

Cogz stores the reasons you choose at a protected app's shield, the time you asked for, whether that time was reached, your optional check-ins (Yes / No), the times you tapped "I don't", your reason and time settings, and your protected-app selection. It is kept in the app's local shared container, excluded from device backups. Apps are represented by random local identifiers and by Apple's opaque tokens, which Cogz uses only to apply its shield. There is no Cogz account, developer server, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or session-replay SDK in this release, and no import or cloud restore.

Screen Time controls

With your authorization, Apple's Family Controls, Managed Settings, Managed Settings UI and Device Activity frameworks let you choose apps, show Cogz' shield, and learn when your chosen amount of use has been reached. Cogz uses this only to help you manage your own device use. It cannot read the contents of messages, websites, other apps, passwords or keystrokes, does not identify apps from their tokens, and never includes tokens in exports. This information is not used for advertising and is not sold or shared with data brokers.

Your Cogz Score

The score is calculated on your device from your own Yes/No check-ins only. Unanswered sessions are not counted. Apple's Screen Time report data is never an input.

Isolated activity reports

The Screen Time totals shown in Cogz are drawn by a separate Apple DeviceActivityReport extension. That extension has no shared App Group, no access to your Cogz history, no networking and no file export. Its numbers stay inside it and never enter your history or score.

Notifications and AI

Cogz sends no notifications unless you turn on "Nudge me when time's up". Then, when a session's time is used up, one local notification asks whether you used the app to do what you wanted to, showing the reason you chose; you can answer Yes or No from it. It never repeats and uses no push server. Cogz uses no AI model, on-device or in the cloud.

Widgets

The optional widget shows today's Cogz Score and counts only (sessions, times you stopped, and waiting check-ins). It never shows app names or reasons.

Purchases

Apple processes optional purchases. Cogz checks Apple-verified entitlement information on your device and never receives your payment-card details. The shield, your history, your score, export and deletion never depend on a purchase.

Exports, support and website

When you export your history or send a support message, the information you choose leaves the app. Exports include your reason names, requested times and check-ins, with random local app IDs instead of app identities; they never include tokens or Screen Time report data. Redacted diagnostics contain counts, versions and error kinds only. Your email provider and the static website host may process correspondence or access logs under their own policies.

Deletion and security

Delete all data clears your local history, settings and app selections and pauses protection. It does not cancel Apple subscriptions or remove files you exported elsewhere. Cogz uses Apple's sandbox, file protection and transactional local storage; no software can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's practices change. Future cloud, analytics or AI processing is not enabled by this policy. Contact the operator with questions or visit support.
