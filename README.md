# InTents

**Use your phone with intention.**

InTents helps you connect your phone use to what you actually want to accomplish. Set priorities for the day, state why you are opening selected apps, use timed intentional sessions, and reflect on whether you followed through.

InTents is designed around a simple idea: **phones can multiply productivity when they are used intentionally.**

### Common issues

**App protection is not working**

Make sure Screen Time access has been granted to InTents and that the apps you want to protect are selected inside InTents.

**I need to regain access to an app**

Use InTents' built-in **Pause Protection** control. Access to recovery controls is not dependent on a paid subscription.

**My session timer kept running after I stopped actively using my phone**

The session timer measures **elapsed time since you intentionally started the session**. It is not a measurement of continuous foreground screen activity.

**My break timer does not prove I stayed off my phone**

Correct. Breaks in InTents are intentionally started by the user. The timer measures elapsed time since the break was started and should not be interpreted as verified phone-free time.

# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 9/19/26**

InTents is designed to collect as little information as possible.

## Data stored by InTents

InTents may store information you create in the app, including:

* daily priorities
* session purposes
* stopping conditions
* return actions
* session and break timestamps
* reflections
* session reviews
* saved purposes
* app settings

This information is stored locally on your device unless a future version explicitly introduces an optional synchronization feature.

## Screen Time access

InTents uses Apple-provided Screen Time technologies, including Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity, to support intentional access to apps selected by the user.

InTents does not use these APIs for advertising or data brokerage.

Apple may represent selected applications using privacy-preserving identifiers provided by the operating system.

## What InTents does not do

The current version of InTents does not:

* sell your personal information
* use advertising trackers
* use third-party behavioral analytics SDKs
* send your priorities or reflections to an AI service
* read the contents of messages, emails, websites, or other apps
* collect passwords or authentication credentials
* operate a developer-owned cloud database containing your journal
* use Screen Time information for advertising

## Purchases

If you purchase an optional InTents subscription or other paid feature, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store.

InTents receives only the information necessary to determine whether access to purchased features is active. InTents does not receive your complete payment-card information.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, InTents may schedule local notifications on your device.

Notification permission is optional.

## Widgets and Live Activities

If you enable widgets or Live Activities, InTents may display information from your sessions or priorities outside the main app.

Where supported, InTents provides settings intended to reduce the amount of potentially sensitive text displayed outside the app.

## Data export and deletion

InTents provides controls for exporting and deleting information stored by the app.

Deleting your local InTents data removes the journal information stored by the app on that device, subject to normal operating-system backup behavior outside the app's control.

## Children

InTents is not designed specifically for children and is not intended to collect personal information from children.

## Security

InTents relies on Apple's application sandbox and operating-system security features to protect local app data.

No software system can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as InTents changes.

If a future version introduces cloud synchronization, analytics, artificial intelligence processing, or other forms of data collection not described here, this policy will be updated accordingly.
